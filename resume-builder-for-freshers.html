Writing a resume with no experience

Recruiters scan a fresher resume for proof that you can build things. Put Education first, then Projects, and keep everything on one page.

What to include

Two to four projects, each with what you built, the tools you used and one measurable result. Add internships, hackathons, certifications and club roles under Work Experience or Activities.

What to leave out

Skip photos, full home addresses and an objective statement. A two-line summary of your strongest skills works better.
